## How do we minimize disruption during the transition?

We minimize disruption by adopting an iterative approach that migrates tests in logical batches. Your team can continue releasing software while we systematically upgrade the test suite.
![iterative transition process](/static/images/iterative-transition-process.avif)

Migrating a large test suite might seem daunting at first glance. We break the process down into manageable phases to reduce disruption to your delivery cycle. The initial phase focuses on the most critical paths and high-value test cases. Subsequent phases migrate the remaining tests while your team continues to release new features. This iterative approach guarantees that your quality gates remain intact throughout the transition. We establish comprehensive reporting and monitoring mechanisms from day one. ## Frequently Asked Questions


  ### How long does a typical migration project take?
  A typical migration project takes anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on the size of your test suite. We provide a detailed timeline after our initial assessment phase.

  ### Do we need to rewrite all our tests from scratch?
  You do need to rewrite the test code to match the new syntax and paradigms. However, the underlying business logic and test scenarios remain the same.

  ### Will our developers receive training during the migration?
  Your developers will receive extensive training and pair programming sessions throughout the migration process. We ensure they are fully comfortable with the new tools.

  ### Can we migrate tests incrementally while keeping the old suite running?
  You can run both test suites side by side during the transition period. We configure your continuous integration pipeline to handle this dual execution smoothly.
