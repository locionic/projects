# Quantum Computing algorithms for developers

Quantum computing is moving out of the realm of theoretical physics and into the hands of software developers. While practical, fault-tolerant quantum computers are still on the horizon, the algorithms that will run on them are already being developed and tested on simulators and noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices.

## Beyond Classical Computing
Classical computers use bits (0 or 1). Quantum computers use qubits, which can exist in a superposition of states and can be entangled with one another. This allows quantum algorithms to explore massive solution spaces simultaneously, solving certain problems exponentially faster than classical algorithms.

## Key Algorithms
Two of the most famous quantum algorithms are Shor's algorithm (for factoring large numbers, threatening modern encryption) and Grover's algorithm (for searching unsorted databases with quadratic speedup). For everyday developers, understanding these principles is crucial for the post-quantum cryptography transition and for identifying problems in their domain that might benefit from quantum speedups in the future.
