# Advanced GraphQL federation

GraphQL federation is the natural evolution for organizations that have outgrown a single, monolithic GraphQL schema. As engineering teams grow, maintaining a unified schema becomes a bottleneck. Federation allows multiple teams to maintain their own independent GraphQL subgraphs, which are then composed into a single, unified supergraph by a gateway.

## The Power of the Supergraph
With federation, the client still queries a single endpoint and experiences the API as a cohesive whole. Behind the scenes, the federated gateway intelligently routes portions of the query to the appropriate subgraphs. This declarative composition is incredibly powerful.

## Resolving Entities Across Subgraphs
One of the most complex aspects of federation is entity resolution. An entity (like a `User` or `Product`) might have its fields spread across multiple subgraphs. For example, the `Users` subgraph might own the user's name and email, while the `Reviews` subgraph owns the reviews written by that user. The gateway must resolve these cross-graph relationships efficiently without overwhelming the underlying services with N+1 query problems.

Advanced federation techniques involve careful schema design, robust caching strategies, and sophisticated query planning at the gateway level to ensure optimal performance and reliability in distributed architectures.
